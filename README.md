# 🏂 Gingerbread Shred

You're a gingerbread man on a snowboard. Show off your moves. Dodge obstacles. Don't lose your head.

🌲 Jump & dodge obstacles\
🚠 Launch off ramps\
🏂 Grind rails\
⛄ Score points for tricks\
🍪 Don't lose your head
